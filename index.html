Book Presentation

Deep Water

Understanding what the story is really about

02 / 09
Book Project by Colton Reinhart

About the Book

Deep Water was written by Maren Stoffels.

The story follows young people trapped in a dangerous situation, where fear, secrets, and difficult choices shape their lives.
03 / 09
Main Idea

What is the story mostly about?

Characters Several young people are forced to deal with fear, danger, and one another.
Conflict The characters must survive a threatening situation while facing hidden secrets.
Message The story shows how people respond when they are pushed into extreme circumstances.
04 / 09
Main Characters

Who is important in the story?

Vesper Main character Vesper is one of the main characters. The book never directly tells us what he did, but throughout it slowly hints that what he did most of the time has been pretty bad.
Gwen Main character Gwen is one of the main characters. She is blind and claims to be innocent throughout the book while still keeping a lot about what she did secret.
Jonah Main character Jonah is also one of the main characters. He seems to be the strongest and oldest person in the group, but while on the ship, he struggles with his roommate, Jumper.
05 / 09
Character Focus

Vesper

Vesper is mysterious because the story does not directly explain what he did to end up in No Exit.

However, the details surrounding him suggest that his past is serious. This makes the reader question whether Vesper can be trusted and what he may be hiding.

06 / 09
Character Focus

Gwen

Gwen is a blind girl who is one of the only innocent girls on the ship.

Her innocence creates a contrast with the darker backgrounds and secrets of the other characters. Gwen helps show how dangerous the situation really is.

07 / 09
Character Focus

Jonah

Jonah is seen as one of the stronger characters in the group.

Even though he appears strong, Jonah is still struggling with his roommate, Jumper. This shows that strength does not mean a person is free from stress or fear.

08 / 09
Finding the Theme

Look for repeated ideas.

Secrets Fear Choices Trust Consequences
Ask yourself: “What do the characters reveal about themselves when they are under pressure?”
09 / 09
Final Thought

Stories entertain us, but they also teach us.

In Deep Water, the characters show that people can have hidden pasts, personal struggles, and different ideas of strength and innocence.

The deeper we look, the more we understand the characters.
