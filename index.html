The Theme or Main Idea of the Story

Understanding what a story is really about

02 / 07
What Is a Theme?

The theme is the message behind the story.

It is the lesson, idea, or belief that the author wants readers to understand after reading.

03 / 07
Main Idea

What is the story mostly about?

Characters Who is involved in the story?
Events What important things happen?
Message What can we learn from it?
04 / 07
Finding the Theme

Look for repeated ideas.

Problems Choices Consequences Changes

Ask yourself: “What does the character learn by the end?”

05 / 07
Example Themes

Common messages in stories

Friendship Good friends support each other.
Courage Being brave means facing difficulties.
Honesty Truthfulness builds trust.
06 / 07
Think About It

Ask these questions while reading.

1 What problem does the character face?
2 How does the character respond?
3 What lesson does the story teach?
07 / 07
Final Thought

Stories entertain us, but they also teach us.

When you identify the theme, you understand the deeper meaning of the story.

Every story has something important to say.
